Mother's maiden name: both 1946 sources (this DP card and the GFH testimony, ../infocenters-gfh/gfh_4229_szluker_moshe_testimony.md) give "Perec", whereas Moshe's own 1999 Yad Vashem Page of Testimony gives "Primer" (father Iacov Primer, mother Haia). Conflict recorded, not resolved: the 1999 page was signed by Moshe as an adult and is accepted as the family name; "Perec" is probably the 13-year-old's confusion with the surname of his paternal grandfather Peretz (YV 9991603: Iacov Shluker's father is Peretz) — **ככל הנראה**.

Facts added about the cousin (for the report): after liberation in the Luboml area he was in Lublin children's home (YV testimony O.3-12647) → Germany: DP registration 21.8.1946, Lindenfels Children's Center (Bergstraße, Hesse), assembly centre 76; June 1947 still in the Hotels Viktoria/Odenwald list; left Lindenfels for Palestine 12.9.1948 (aliyah 1948 in his testimony). Tracing file ("Kinderakte") sent to Israel 12.10.1951.
