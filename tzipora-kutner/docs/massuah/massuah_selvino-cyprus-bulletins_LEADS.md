## Why it matters
These are the only contemporary voices of the Selvino group during the months (Mar–Sep 1947) in which the report places both Tzipora Blam and Avraham Kutner in the Cyprus camps (ship "Ch. Arlozorof" deported 24.3.1947; Tzipora: "7 months in Cyprus", group of 75 children; Esther Werber: reached Atlit 25.9.1947). The 6.6.1947 issue is the right period.
