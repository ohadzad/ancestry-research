Date 31.I.1946; Assembly Center No. T.165, LEIPHEIM; (23) Remarks: Block 24"; stamp "UNRRA TEAM 165 LEIPHEIM-CENTRE"; handwritten at top left "13.V.1946" (purpose not stated: processing/issue date of this copy) and registration no. "G 112 18190" (as I read it).
  - **New facts:** (a) Rachel's own signature at 16 and the registration date at Leipheim (31 January 1946; she is registered there already in Jan 1946, so she reached Germany by then — the report's "בית בני עקיבא בבודפשט ... אחר כך גרמניה" fits); (b) she gave her parents as **father "Fazekas Leib" and mother née "Strulowitz Rosa"** — i.e. the surnames are the reverse of the report's reading (father Leib STRULOVITS, mother Rifka-Rose née FARKAS; Stutthof questionnaire "Lajos S., Rose Farkas"; civil birth record "Struljovic Leba / Farkas Rifka"). In January 1946 she herself (or the clerk) put "Fazekas/Farkas" as the family name of the father and "Strulowitz" as the mother's maiden name. This looks like the Carpathian practice recorded in the report (children of religious-only marriages carried the mother's surname Farkas, and 'Strulovits' was the father's), mis-sorted by the registrar; but it is a contemporaneous statement by Rachel and must be reconciled in the report's name chapter (chapter א: "שמות"). "Fazekas" is a distinct Hungarian name (potter), the clerk's rendering of Farkas.
  - Also: the card's top-left "13.V.1946" is the only "May 1946" date in the Arolsen material; if it is the date of her departure/clearing from Leipheim it supports the YV summary "aliyah May 1946" and the April 1947 lists below would then be stale name lists; if not, her aliyah was later. Open.

## Why this matters (conflicts with the report)
1. **Rachel's aliyah year.** The report states "עלייה 05/1946" (Yad Vashem catalogue summary of her 2009 testimony: "עלייה לארץ ישראל במאי 1946"; in the transcript she gives no year) and builds on it the argument that she could not have been in Cyprus (camps opened Aug 1946). Arolsen shows Rachel and Gedalia as inhabitants of Leipheim in April 1947 (typed list dated by its own content). Either the YV summary has the wrong year (aliyah May 1947 or later), or the list is mis-dated. Needs reconciliation: check the 1949 voter register entry date, the Mikveh Israel school/youth records and the ship/arrival date.
2. **Gedaliyahu's "Cyprus ship" identification.** The report (ch. ז') identifies him as "פארקאש גדליהו, 32, בודאפסט" on the list of the 806 deportees of "HaMa'apil HaAlmoni" (captured 17.2.1947 and deported to Cyprus) and calls the identification כמעט ודאי. But Farkas Gedalia b. 3.3.1931 (Wolowoje) is in Leipheim in April 1947, i.e. after that capture. Either he was not on that ship (the Cyprus entry may be another boy — note there is a "Fazekas Gedalia, b. 4.8.1930, Budapest" in the same Leipheim block, adjacent in the lists, a likelier "Budapest" candidate), or the Leipheim list still carried him after he left. His Atlit record (doc 28052) can still be real for a later ship (summer 1947-48).
3. The family-memory of "Cyprus" may therefore attach to Gedaliyahu's later Aliyah Bet voyage, not to February 1947.
Grades: presence of both siblings in Leipheim in 1947 — מאומת (two independent Arolsen sources, plus the existing CNI card); the implied aliyah year and the Cyprus identification — טעון אימות.
