# DEGOB protocol 793 — two Schneider women of Ökörmező: Szeklence -> Auschwitz -> Stutthof -> Praust airfield -> evacuation 15 Jan 1945 -> Putzig -> liberation by Soviet tanks

- Source: https://degob.hu/index.php?showjk=793 (scan https://degob.hu/index.php?showjk_img=793), degob_get_protocol(793), retrieved 2026-10-27. German text under a Hungarian header; taken down by "Lilly Blau"; the text ends with the signatures "Schneider Aranka, Schneider Relly" (full names are NOT anonymised in the body, unlike the header).
- Header (verbatim): S. A. — nő, Ökörmezö, 1929, tanuló, Utolsó lakóhely Ökörmezö, Gettó Szeklence, Táborok Auschwitz, Stutthof, Praust; S. R. — nő, Ökörmezö, 1926, szabó, same chain.
- Key verbatim: "Am 15. April 1944 wurden wir aus unseren Wohnungen von den ungarischen Gendarmen in den Tempel unseres Dorfes gebracht ... drei Tage" / "Im Szeklencer Ghetto ..." / "Man presste 80 Menschen in einen Waggon, ohne Wasser, ohne Luft und nach drei Tagen kamen wir in Auschwitz an" / mother and two sisters kept with her at the Stutthof selection by asking the camp doctor / "Man führte uns ins Prauster Lager. Wir bauten hier einen Flugplatz" / "So lebten wir bis zum 15. Jaenner dann begannen wir zu evakuieren" / "Wir kamen nach Putzig ... Am Tag vorher hatte man dort 12.000 Häftlinge eingeschifft und versenkt" / liberated by Russian tanks / "von den 800 Frauen, die von Praus abmarschiert waren nur 150 erleben".
- Cross-checks: the Arolsen Stutthof cohort (docs/arolsen/arolsen_okormezo_born_stutthof_cohort_index.md) has SNEIDER Rozsi b. 28.8.1926 no. 37969 (= S. R. b. 1926, "Relly/Rozsi"), SCHNEIDER Aranka b. 3.6.1923 no. 37966 and SCHNEIDER Franciska b. 23.6.1921 no. 37968 (S. A. b. 1929 does not match Aranka's 1923 - maybe a different Aranka or an age shift; Franciska could be the older sister the protocol mentions). Possible link to Rita (née Schneider) Pollack, the Ökörmező child-witness cited in the report (KehilaLinks): her description (gathered 16.4.1944 at 7 a.m., 80 per wagon, arrival Auschwitz 19.5) overlaps strongly with this protocol; "Relly" might be Rita - **טעון אימות**.
- Value for Rachel: an independent 1945-46 account of the exact Praust -> Putzig evacuation path of Ökörmező women from the same 29.6.1944 Stutthof transport (numbers 37966-37969 vs Rachel 38444) and of the 15 January 1945 start date; the report notes that the column in which Rachel marched is unidentified (Drywa documents Grodno->Lauenburg and Chorab->Koronowo but not Praust). Rachel's own testimony (Thorn, ~1,200 marched, ~500 arrived) differs; no mention of Strulovits in this protocol.
- Grade: ככל הנראה (context/parallel only; no family link).
