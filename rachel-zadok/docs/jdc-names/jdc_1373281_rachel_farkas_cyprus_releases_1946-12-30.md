- Why it is checked: the report discusses a family tradition that Rachel stayed in a Cyprus camp and rejects it (chapter ד', "המסורת על מחנה מעבר בקפריסין"), attaching Cyprus instead to Gedaliyahu. This Farkas Rachel was an internee to be released in Jan 1947; since Rachel Zadok is documented in Leipheim on 31.1.1946 (DP-2 card) and in April 1947 (lists), a Cyprus internee in Jan 1947 cannot be her; the entry is most likely one of the many Polish/Romanian Farkas women on the list. No age to test. Grade: נשלל (not her) — with reason: the Leipheim documents place Rachel in Germany on the dates around it; לא נבדק מול גיל.
