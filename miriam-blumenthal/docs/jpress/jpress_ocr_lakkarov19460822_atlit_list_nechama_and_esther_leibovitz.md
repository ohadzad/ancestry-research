## The list's own heading (summary)
The title: "מעפילי 'החייל העברי' ו'הגנה' שהועברו למחנה עתלית" ("The ma'apilim of 'HaHayal HaIvri' and 'Haganah' who were moved to the Atlit camp"). The introduction says that the previous issue printed a partial list (1,700) of the "Haganah" ma'apilim, and that this issue prints the remainder of that ship's list and the full list of "HaHayal HaIvri"; the entries give "שם המשפחה, השם הפרטי, הגיל, שמות ההורים ... וארץ המוצא" ("surname, given name, age, parents' names ... and country of origin"), the country as an initial, among them "ה — הונגריה" ("ה — Hungary") and "ר — רומניה" ("ר — Romania").

## What it shows
- Text: מאומת (as OCR). "פרימאם" is OCR; the certificates of both sisters print the mother as "פרימט".
- Two daughters of Moshe and Frimet(?) Leibovitz from Hungary reached Atlit in the summer of 1946, on one of the two ships named in the title; the list does not say which.
- Esther, 28 in August 1946 (born about 1918), against 32 on her marriage certificate of October 1948 (born 1915/16): ages on these lists are often approximate. That this Esther is the Esther of certificate H 99913 and of the Pages of Testimony: ככל הנראה (same given name, same parents' names, Hungary).
- Nechama, 21 (born about 1925): a sister unknown to the project, or Miriam under another given name (Miriam was born in 1925 according to the voters' list of 1949). אפשר ש… (טעון אימות) for either reading. Miriam herself is not on the list; the ל-block of the list is present in the OCR.

## Addendum, edition 9 (27.10.2026): which ship
- The Aliyah Department's own lists in Haifa (Israel State Archives, via IGRA) settle it: the "Balboa–Haganah" list of 27.7.1946 has "לייבוביץ אסתר 26" and "לייבוביץ מגדה 21", both from Hungary, and no Nechama; the HaHayal HaIvri list of 31.7.1946 has no Leibovitz from Hungary. Esther of this list came on the "Haganah": probable. See [the transcription of the Haifa lists](../igra/haifa_aliyah_lists_1946_haganah_and_hahayal-haivri_leibovitz_transcription.md).
- "Nechama" and "Magda": probably one woman. The two lists render names differently: "אוה", 20, from Hungary on the ship's list is "ליבוביץ חוה 20, דאז׳י אטלקה, ה" in this paper's list of 6.8.1946. And "נחמה" here is OCR, in a line whose mother's name the OCR also garbles ("פרימאם").
- The previous issue (6.8.1946, `lakkarov19460806-01.2.2`) was read again in full for this addendum: its Leibovitz rows have no Moshe + Frima, no Magda, no Nechama, and no Avraham aged 13 from Hungary (the ship's list has one).
