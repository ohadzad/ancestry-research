## Re-grading (27.10.2026, lead researcher) — the exclusion above is withdrawn
- "Mór" is the standard Hungarian civil form of **Moshe** — so the father's name **matches** the marriage certificate (משה הלוי ליבוביץ). The exclusion's premise ("parents' names differ") is wrong for the father.
- Mother: "Fanni L., geb. Vegh" (typed on the green Buchenwald card, docs/arolsen/lebovits_magda_1922_file/arolsen_7638678_01_front.jpg; handwritten "Fani geb Vegh" on the personal card). Miriam's certificate: **פרימט**. Hungarian Jews often took a civil name sharing the initial of the Yiddish name, so Fanni for Frimet is possible — not shown.
- Birth year 1922 vs 1925/26 (1949 voters list; marriage certificate age 22; MyHeritage tree 1925). Father "schuster" vs "סוחר" on YV page 800983 (Moshe Lebowits, Balassagyarmat).
- For: "Magda" is Miriam's name in the family's MyHeritage tree; Lebovits; Balassagyarmat (the town of the only Moshe+Frima Lebowits couple in Yad Vashem); father Mór = Moshe.
- **Grade: אפשר ש... — טעון אימות.** Questions that decide it: Miriam's birthday (20 January?), her mother's maiden name (Végh?), Ravensbrück / Altenburg in her story, a relative "Rose/Rózsi" in Budapest.
