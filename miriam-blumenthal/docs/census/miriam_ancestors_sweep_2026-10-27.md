# Miriam Blumenthal (née Leibovitz / Lebovics) - sweep for the generation before her parents - 27.10.2026

Scope: Moshe (Mór) Lebovics (b. ~1888-1892) and his parents Avraham + Feige-Toibe; Frumet / Franciska Végh and her parents Avraham + Olga; their marriage; early children's births; Moshe's brother Yitzhak of Magdiel.
Not edited: report.md, sources-index.md, build.py, any page. Only this file, three Yad Vashem scans in `docs/candidates/`, and two evidence images in `docs/census/img/` were added.
Read first: report.md section 4.1, `docs/census/lebovits_balassagyarmat_censuses_registers_2026-10-27.md`, section 9.4.

## Bottom line

New, and it moves the project one generation up on Moshe's side:

1. **Moshe's parents lived in Alsóapsa (Máramaros county, Szigeti district; today Nyzhnya Apsha / Dolní Apša, Rachov district).** Three more Pages of Testimony by the same Yitzhak (same Hebrew signature, same date 8.1.57, same Magdiel address as the Moshe page 800983) name Moshe's siblings Zeev (Wolf), Frida (married Davidovits), and Zeev's wife Ester, all of Alsóapsa, with the same parents Avraham and Feige-Toibe. This also explains why David gave Shimshon's birth (1914) in Alsóapsa.
2. **JewishGen "Máramaros Vital Records Births" (index rows from Baia Mare archive, Register Book 281) shows a complete sibling set born in Alsóapsa to Abraham Lebovits and Feige Tobe HOCH:** Mozes Gedalya 28.4.1887, Volf 31.5.1889, Hers 18.3.1892, Iczik Jakob 22.11.1894. Mother's maiden name **Hoch** (home town Fehéregyháza) is new. Moshe's date (1887) differs by about 3 years from every other source (1890) - see finding 2.
3. **IGRA confirms Yitzhak's identity and parents from a second, independent angle:** IGRA-1950-69-Immigration-16726, "Yitzhak Leibovitz, child of Avraham & Tova", b. 1894, from Hungary, Shaar Haaliyah, 26.5.1950 - matches Iczik Jakob b. 22.11.1894 in the register.
4. **Végh side: nothing confirmed.** One lead (a Végh Abraham, son of Simsi, of Alsóapsa, married to a Lebovits woman) is recorded as a possibility only; the mother's name does not match "Olga".
5. **Not found:** Moshe x Végh marriage (1910-1914); Shimshon's 1914 birth; Esther's birth; any record of Avraham/Feige-Toibe after 1894 (death, 1921 census); Fani's own birth.

What it identifies, and proposed grade: **ככל הנראה (likely) - not higher: these are index rows; the register scans were not seen.**
- The couple Abraham x Feige Tobe in Alsóapsa matches, name for name, the parents on Yitzhak's pages (Avraham; Feiga + Toibe), and matches the 3 siblings of F1: Zeev = Volf; Yitzhak = Iczik Jakob (also F3). That makes the match to the couple strong.
- **Mozes Gedalya = our Moshe?** Name matches (Mozes = Moshe) and parents match, but the date does not: 28.4.1887 gives age 57 in 1944, while Esther's pages (1956) and Yitzhak's page give 1890 and the Nógrád archive book says 54. Possible explanations: (i) a 3-year error in the family's memory; (ii) an unindexed child born between Volf (May 1889) and Hers (March 1892) who is the real Moshe, while Mozes Gedalya (1887) died young (no death row for him was found, but the deaths index checked here is only partial); (iii) a different family. **Treat as "likely the family, undecided on the child"** until the register image is read. One interesting detail: Moshe's first name could have been Gedalya (Mozes Gedalya) - no other source gives a second name.
- Mother's maiden name **Hoch** and her home town Fehéregyháza (spelled Fejeregyhaza / Fehéregyháza in the rows; one row gives "Also Apsa") are new for the family.
- Godfather Gedalyovits Ancsel (1887) and Gedalyovits Motye (1892) suggest a Gedalyovits relative (the name Gedalya for the 1887 child).
- The sibling set is consistent with F1: Zeev/Volf, Frida (a daughter of this couple is not in the index - she would be born about 1896-1899; the index has none under this surname/town; unresolved), Yitzhak, Hers (Hers b. 1892 is not on any page seen; possibly the one who died or emigrated - open).

The marriage Moshe x Végh could just as well be with a Végh from another Máramaros village. Proposed grade: **אפשר ש… (possible), not for use in the report except as a lead.**
Excluded: Eszter Lebovits née Veig / Veigh, b. 1891 Alsóapsa (YV 1828708, submitter Khana Veig, daughter) - parents Mendl/Hers and Ida, spouse Marton: a different Végh family. ## (c) Blockers

1. **FamilySearch** - no token. It is the only route to the **images** of the Alsóapsa/Szigeti civil register (Baia Mare archive, Register Book 281, rows 56-10, 64-23, 75-14, 128-21; the 1910s marriage and birth books of Alsóapsa) and of the Máramaros/Zakarpattia civil registers. All F2 grades stay "likely" until those images are read.
2. **JewishGen MCP detail pages** - `jewishgen_search_filtered` on a result URL returned "Scanned 0 rows" and `jewishgen_open` returned "0 matching record" although the overview shows hits (known quirk). Treated as a blocker, not a negative. Worked around by running the same search in the logged-in Chrome (Global Search form, then "List n records"). The `jgdetail_2.php` URLs from the MCP also return 0 in the browser.
3. **IGRA full record and scan** of 16726 - details only for IGRA members; the image URL answered HTTP 403 and was not pursued (no bypass). Only the list line (Hebrew + English) was captured. Magdiel as address on that row is therefore not verified.
4. **RadixIndex** Verlustliste "Lebovics - Alsoapsa" (WW1 casualty list) and RadixRef Balassagyarmat rows - "record retrieval for subscribers".
5. **MACSE** record-level content (member account).
6. **Yad Vashem story endpoint** error HTTP 523 on all records read (no effect on the data fields).
7. The Hungaricana library images of the 1921 census, Alsóapsa (`KANepszaml_012_Nagybocsko_Alsoapsa__162_Nyizsnya-Apsa`) - scans sit behind the site's viewer; not browsed (could contain Abraham's household under another spelling).
