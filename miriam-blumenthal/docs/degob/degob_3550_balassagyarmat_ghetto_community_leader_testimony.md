# DEGOB protocol 3550 — Balassagyarmat ghetto and deportation (context for Benő & Ida)

- URL: https://degob.hu/index.php?showjk=3550 (scan viewer: https://degob.hu/index.php?showjk_img=3550). Hungarian original, 23,685 chars read (first 20,000).
- Survivor (anonymised): "dr. Z. Gy.", male, b. 1908 Balassagyarmat, lawyer, last residence Balassagyarmat, ghetto Balassagyarmat, camps Auschwitz, Mauthausen, Melk, Ebensee. Former member of the Jewish Council; at recording, president of the community.
- Not about the Blumenthals by name. Grade for the family: **ככל הנראה** (as the chronology of the ghetto they were in — "גיארמת (גטו)" on Yehuda Blumenthal's Yad Vashem pages) — NOT a record of them.

## Verbatim facts (Hungarian, key sentences; translation by me)
- "A balassagyarmati zsidóság létszáma 2400 volt" — Balassagyarmat Jewry numbered 2,400.
- "1944. május első hetében 10-ig kellett a gettót megalakítani" — ghetto formed by 10 May 1944; a small town ghetto ("két és fél utca") plus a separate small block for **"a vidéki zsidóság"** (the rural Jews — the category Nógrádbercel's Jews belong to); "Május 10-én hozták be a falusi zsidóságot".
- "Május második felében háromszor vagy négyszer volt a gettóban sorozás, amelynek eredményeként az összes egészséges 16-48 éves férfiakat elvitték munkaszolgálatra" — in late May, healthy men aged **16-48** were taken from the ghetto to labour service; the ghetto kept children, men over 48, women and the sick. (Benő b. 1888/92 = 52-56: stayed; Yaakov b. ~1918, if he was in the ghetto, would have been taken.)
- Early June: night order by gendarmes, 30 minutes to leave with 50 kg; moved to **Nyírjespuszta**, two big tobacco barns, **2,000-2,500 people, of whom ~1,850 from Gyarmat and ~700-800 from the district ("vidéki")**, "a munkaszolgálatosok nélkül".
- "Június 10-én ... vagoníroztak": ~**2,800** persons in one transport (Balassagyarmat town + Balassagyarmat district + ~100 from Losonc); ~70 per wagon. Kassa: Germans took over.
- "Június 14-én hajnalban érkeztünk meg Auschwitz-Birkenauba." — arrival at dawn 14 June 1944 (= museum transport 308, arrival 14-06-1944, 1867 deported; see docs/auschwitz). Men and women separated; healthy men 16-60 selected; the rest "senki sem látta" — presumably gassed.
- Jewish Council (first): Lázár Mihály (president), Sándor Dezső and Pál, dr. Hajdú Ferenc, dr. Léván Imre, Weltner Jakab; second: Jónás Emil, dr. Zilczer György, Deutsch József (chief rabbi), Révész Márton.
Note: the survivor puts the number leaving on 10 June at ~2,800, vs museum 2,810 (transport 309) and 1,867 (transport 308); two trains may have left Balassagyarmat 10-12 June.

**עדכון 27.10.2026 (ביקורת) — על הטרנספורט.** בתקציר שלמעלה נכתב שההגעה ב-14.6 "= טרנספורט 308" (1,867 איש). הדוח אינו מזהה כך: התאריכים 12.6 ו-14.6 ברשימת גאשקו (מוזיאון אושוויץ) הם מועדי המעבר בקאשה, לא מועדי ההגעה; והרכבת שתיאר העד — כ-2,800 איש, יצאה ב-10.6 והגיעה ב-14.6 — מתאימה לזו של 2,810 (12.6 בקאשה). ראו דוח יעקב בלומנטל 4.7.
