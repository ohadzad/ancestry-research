## Summary
Nothing new about Avraham came out of the camp and DP databases: Auschwitz has 20 people under the Kutner, Kuttner and Mlotek spellings and none under Bandus or any given-name pair of the family; Arolsen shows 7 rows for 133332 (two being the known Mauthausen card), 73 rows across the name queries, none for Cywia, and no Abram from Ozorków or Łódź other than the card; JDC has 47 exact Kutners and the one Abram on the 1946 Italian aid list (person f4184e23…, with Sara as e5ca81b0…), still without a birth year; Pinpoint gives 49 document-level OCR matches that were not opened; the Ghetto Fighters' House (93 and 86 hits) and Massuah (24 and 3) hold only the two known Selvino items and unrelated Kutners. Two Arolsen rows are new to the project and both are context, not evidence about Avraham: another Sala Kutner (b. 1920) on the Łódź list material, and Dachau no. 110139 (Israel Kutner b. 1912 "Alexandrov"), which probably explains the Dachau-liberated Israel Kutner of the July 1945 press and so leaves no. 96029 as the open candidate.
