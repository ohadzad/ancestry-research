# Arolsen 87764789 — ITS letter about Gross-Rosen sub-camps Mittelstein and Grafenort, index entry "KUTNER SALA"

- **Source:** Arolsen 1.1.0.7 "Compilation of Information Regarding Various Detention Sites and Camps" > "Concentration camp Groß-Rosen and commands" (ref. 1107011, 350 docs). https://collections.arolsen-archives.org/en/document/87764789 — 2 scans (front/back) saved here.
- **What the scan is:** typed letter to ITS, DP camp Föhrenwald, dated 23.2.1950 (year partly illegible), from Pola Jastrzębska (now Rewisowska, KZ no. 55041), describing forced labour in the Gross-Rosen sub-camps Mittelstein and Grafenort (opened 23.8.44, closed March 45; ~400 women). Fellow prisoners list; item 7 reads "Sala ?[...]ner  ?" — the middle of the surname is destroyed by a hole in the page; the Arolsen indexer typed it as KUTNER.
- **Grade: טעון אימות (probably not Sala b. 1922).** Reason: Sala Kutner's Buchenwald personal file (docs/arolsen/Sala-Kutner-Buchenwald-1861737) records Auschwitz 10.8.44 -> Bergen-Belsen -> Buchenwald 18.1.45, no Gross-Rosen stage; the letter's woman is anonymous (no number, no birth date). Keep only as a lead: if Sala did pass Mittelstein/Grafenort the Buchenwald card would be incomplete. Useful context only: Aug 1944 Auschwitz women were sent to these sub-camps.
