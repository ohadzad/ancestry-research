## Why it is NOT established as Avraham's father Izrael (report 3.1/4.1)
- Father in the Lodz Ghetto List: KUTNER Izrael, b. **18/09/1897**, "Origin: Ozorkow", Konigsberger Str. 4 flat 35, reg. 26/08/1942, "Arbeiter". Witness page: b. 1892 Strykow, grain merchant.
- Same JewishGen Lodz Ghetto List (read 2026-10-27, page 101-150 of the 303 Kutner rows) contains a different **KUTNER, Izrael, b. 13/11/1900**, "Lodz, Poland" (prior address Poludniowa), Alexanderhof Strasse 38 flat 20 — day and month (13.11) equal the Dachau card exactly, Litzmannstadt-born matches "Litzmannstadt". The Dachau man is therefore at least as likely this Izrael (year 1897 vs 1900 = clerical difference).
- Occupation "Buchhalter" does not match "Arbeiter"/"grain merchant"; place of birth "Litzmannstadt" does not match Strykow/Ozorkow.
- For: first name+surname, Łódź ghetto, Auschwitz->Dachau 1.9.1944 fits the Łódź liquidation (Auschwitz DB transports from Łódź 24.8, 30.8, 2.9.1944), year 1897 as in the ghetto list.
- Resolve with: Dachau/Kaufering death register entry for 2.11.1944 (Arolsen 1.1.6.x; Totenbuch), ITS T/D file for Izrael Kutner, or the Pages of Testimony submitter.
