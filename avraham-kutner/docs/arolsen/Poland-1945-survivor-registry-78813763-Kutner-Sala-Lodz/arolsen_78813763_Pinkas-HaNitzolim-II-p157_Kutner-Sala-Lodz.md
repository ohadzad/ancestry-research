# "Registry of Jewish survivors; list of Jews in Poland 1945" — page 157: "Kutner Sala, Łódź"

- **Source:** Arolsen 3.1.1.3 PL "Registrations of former persecutees in Poland", unit "Registry of Jewish survivors; list of Jews in Poland 1945" (768000, 304 docs). https://collections.arolsen-archives.org/en/document/78813763 (scan arolsen_78813763_01_front.jpg = printed page 157). It is the same printed book as JewishGen "Pinkas HaNitzolim II" (JG0058; compiled by the Central Committee of Polish Jews, Warsaw, 15.8.1945; 57,702 names).
- **Verbatim column 3 of the page:** Kutner Chaja, Lublin · Kutner Dienkle, Bydgoszcz · Kutner Estera, Warszawa · Kutner Ewa, Łódź · Kutner Lajb, Łódź · Kutner Moszek, Łódź · Kutner Roza, Bydgoszcz · **Kutner Sala, Łódź** · Kutner Sara, Bydgoszcz · Kutner Sara, Łódź · Kutner Szlama, Łódź · Kutner Szprynca, Warszawa. No year of birth, no parents.
- **Grade: טעון אימות.** Sala is a common name; but a returning Łódź survivor named Sala Kutner in summer 1945 is plausible for the sister Sala (b. 14.8.1922, Łódź ghetto, Buchenwald file; JewishGen Bergen-Belsen index also lists KUTNER Sala 14.08.1922 Lodz). No Abram/Israel/Cywia on the page.
